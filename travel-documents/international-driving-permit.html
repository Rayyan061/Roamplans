International Driving Permit Guide: Do You Need an IDP Abroad?

✦ IDP requirements✓ Official U.S. issuers↕ Rental-car document checks

Renting a car abroad can give you more ↗ freedom, but your U.S. driver's license may not be enough in every country. Some destinations accept it on its own, while others require or recognize an International Driving Permit, usually called an IDP. Rental companies can also have document rules that go beyond the country's basic legal requirement.

An IDP can be easy to misunderstand. It is not a replacement for your driver's license, and it does not give you new driving rights. It works alongside your valid domestic license and translates key information so foreign authorities can understand it more easily. The U.S. Department of State tells travelers to carry their U.S. driver's license with the IDP when driving abroad.

When I plan to rent a car internationally, I check the IDP requirement before reserving the vehicle. I look at the destination government's driving rules first, then check the rental company's conditions. This avoids finding out at the rental counter that an extra document was required.

This guide explains what an International Driving Permit is, whether you need one abroad, how U.S. travelers can get one, how long it lasts, and what to check before renting or driving a car overseas.

!
Travel Note

Driving-document requirements vary by country and can change. Check the destination's official driving rules and your rental company's current conditions before travel.

What Is an International Driving Permit?

An International Driving Permit is a travel document that translates information from your domestic driver's license into multiple languages.

It normally includes information such as:

  • ✓
    Your name
  • ✓
    Photograph
  • ✓
    Driver information
  • ✓
    Authorized vehicle categories
  • ✓
    Translations of key license details

AAA says its IDP translates identifying information into 10 languages and is recognized as identification in more than 150 countries.

The main purpose is to make your driving credentials easier for foreign police, rental companies, and other authorities to understand.

That can be especially useful when your driver's license is written only in English and you are traveling somewhere where another language is used.

Is an IDP the Same as an International Driver's License?

People often use the phrase international driver's license, but the official document U.S. travelers should look for is the International Driving Permit.

USA Gov notes that an IDP is sometimes called an international driver's license, but it also warns travelers about unofficial websites claiming to sell international licenses or permits.

This distinction matters because an IDP does not create a separate driver's license.

Your real driving authority still comes from your valid domestic license.

Think of the documents as a pair:

Valid U.S. driver's license + valid IDP

rather than:

IDP instead of driver's license

If a website promises a standalone international license that lets you drive without your normal license, check it very carefully.

Does an IDP Replace Your U.S. Driver's License?

No.

This is one of the most important things to know before traveling.

The State Department specifically tells U.S. travelers with an IDP to carry their U.S. driver's license as well. AATA also states that the IDP is a supplement to the U.S. license rather than a replacement.

If police, a rental company, or another authority asks for driving documents, you may need to show both.

So keep these together while driving:

  • ✓
    Valid domestic driver's license
  • ✓
    International Driving Permit where required
  • ✓
    Passport or identification where required
  • ✓
    Rental documents
  • ✓
    Insurance documents

Do not leave your original driver's license at the hotel because you are carrying an IDP.

Do You Need an International Driving Permit Abroad?

Sometimes.

There is no single answer that applies to every destination.

The State Department says some countries accept a U.S. driver's license, while many others require an IDP.

The correct answer depends on:

  • ✓

    Your driver's license
  • ✓
    Length of stay
  • ✓
    Vehicle type
  • ✓
    Local law
  • ✓
    Rental company
  • ✓
    Sometimes the region within the country

This means the right question is not:

Do Americans always need an IDP abroad?

Instead ask:

Does my destination require or recognize an IDP for someone driving with my U.S. license?

Countries Where a U.S. License May Be Enough

Some destinations accept a valid U.S. driver's license for short-term visitors.

Two simple examples are Canada and Mexico. Current USA Gov guidance says U.S. citizens visiting either country do not need an IDP to drive.

Current State Department information for Canada also says U.S. driver's licenses are valid for visitors.

But these examples should not become a general rule for other countries.

Do not assume:

Canada accepts my license, so Europe, Asia, or South America will too.

Check each destination separately.

Do You Need an IDP If Your U.S. License Is in English?

Possibly.

The IDP is useful as a translation document, but whether it is legally required does not depend only on whether local officials understand English.

Some destinations may require an IDP under their driving-document rules.

Others may accept certain foreign licenses without one.

That means:

My license is in English

is not enough to decide whether you need an IDP?

Check the destination rule.

Who Can Get an IDP in the United States?

AATA's current eligibility information says its U.S. IDPs are issued to people who:

  • ✓
    Are at least 18 years old
  • ✓
    Hold a valid U.S. driver's license

You do not have to be a U.S. citizen, but the IDP must be connected to a valid U.S. license.

This means a foreign visitor holding only a foreign driver's license should not obtain a U.S.-issued IDP to go with that foreign license.

AATA says the IDP should be issued in the same country as the driver's domestic license.

Where Can U.S. Travelers Get an Official IDP?

This is an area where travelers need to be careful.

USA Gov currently identifies two official organizations authorized by the U.S. Department of State to issue IDPs:

  • ✓
    American Automobile Association, AAA
  • ✓
    American Automobile Touring Alliance, AATA

The State Department's own driving-abroad guidance also directs travelers to AAA or AATA before a trip.

You do not need to buy a permit from a random website calling itself an international driving authority.

Start from one of these official U.S. issuers.

Beware of Fake International Driving Permits

IDP scams are common enough that USA Gov specifically warns travelers about websites claiming to issue international driver's licenses or IDPs.

It says many may be fraudulent and could try to collect your money or personal information.

Be suspicious of a company that promises:

  • ✓
    Instant worldwide driver's license
  • ✓
    Digital-only international license
  • ✓
    IDP without a valid domestic driver's license
  • ✓
    Lifetime international permit
  • ✓
    Permit valid for many years without normal rules
  • ✓
    License that replaces your state driver's license

Use the official issuers.

This is especially important because an IDP application involves sensitive information such as your driver's-license details and photograph.

Can You Get a Digital IDP?

Current U.S. issuer guidance says the recognized IDP is a physical document.

AAA states that its IDPs are physically printed and mailed and that digital versions are not available. AATA likewise says it does not issue a digital version or electronic copy.

You may be able to complete an application online, but that does not mean the final permit exists only on your phone.

Plan enough time for the physical document to reach you.

How Long Is an International Driving Permit Valid?

For U.S.-issued IDPs discussed in current USA Gov and AATA guidance, the permit is valid for one year.

It must also be used with a valid U.S. driver's license.

If your domestic license expires or otherwise becomes invalid, having an unexpired IDP does not turn it into a replacement license.

Can You Renew an IDP?

USA Gov says an IDP cannot be renewed. If you need another one after the current permit ends, you obtain a new IDP.

AATA similarly says a new application is needed each year.

Do Not Apply Too Early

AAA currently says an IDP cannot be issued more than six months before your requested effective date.

So you can plan ahead, but there is little reason to apply a year before your trip.

How Much Does an International Driving Permit Cost?

AAA currently lists a $20 permit fee for an IDP.

Extra costs can apply depending on how you apply.

For example, AAA's current online process lists:

  • ✓
    $20 IDP permit fee
  • ✓
    $10 passport-photo fee
  • ✓
    Applicable shipping costs

If you apply in person and already have the required photos, the basic permit fee is currently $20, although AAA advises checking with the branch for exact charges and availability.

AATA also currently lists a $20 IDP fee plus its photo-processing and shipping charges for online applications.

Fees can change, so confirm them when you apply rather than depending on an old travel article.

What Documents Do You Need to Get an IDP?

The exact submission method depends on the issuer.

For an in-person AAA application, current instructions require:

  • ✓
    Completed application
  • ✓
    Two original passport-style photos
  • ✓
    Valid U.S. driver's license
  • ✓
    Permit fee

AAA's current online process instead asks applicants to:

  • ✓
    Complete and sign the application
  • ✓
    Provide a digital passport-style photo
  • ✓
    Capture the front and back of the driver's license
  • ✓
    Pay the applicable fee and shipping cost

AATA's online process also requires a passport-style photo and images of a valid U.S. driver's license.

Do not send sensitive documents through an unofficial site.

Do You Need Your Passport to Apply for an IDP?

An IDP application uses passport-style photos, but that is different from saying every application method requires you to submit your passport.

AAA's listed in-person requirements focus on the application, passport photos, valid U.S. driver's license, and fee.

Check the current issuer instructions for the method you choose.

Your passport will still be an important travel document once you are abroad, but your driving licence is the document the IDP is tied to.

Can You Get an IDP Online?

Yes, current AAA and AATA services allow eligible applicants to start and complete IDP applications online.

However, remember the important difference:

Online application does not mean digital permit.

The physical IDP still has to be produced and delivered.

AAA currently says its online applications generally need about five business days for processing plus return shipping.

AATA currently lists processing of roughly one to four days at normal volumes, with shipping time added separately.

Do not leave the application until the night before your flight.

Can You Apply for an IDP While Already Abroad?

It may still be possible if you hold a valid U.S. driver's license.

AAA currently offers an online application option for travelers who are already overseas, with the physical document shipped after processing.

AATA also states that a holder of a valid U.S. driver's license can apply while outside the United States.

Still, getting it before departure is much easier.

If you wait until after arrival, you may face:

  • ✓
    Shipping delays
  • ✓
    International delivery costs
  • ✓
    Hotel-address issues
  • ✓
    Rental reservation problems
  • ✓
    Days when you cannot legally or contractually drive

The State Department recommends getting the IDP before your trip.

Can a Foreign Country Issue an IDP for Your U.S. License?

You should obtain an IDP from the country connected to your domestic driver's license.

AAA states that another country cannot issue an IDP to accompany a U.S. driver's license and that an IDP used with a U.S. license should be issued in the United States.

AATA gives the same general rule: an IDP should be issued in the same country as the driver's domestic license.

This is another reason not to buy a permit from a random overseas website after arriving.

Europe Does Not Have One IDP Rule

One of the easiest mistakes is thinking there is a single driver's-license rule for all of Europe.

There is not.

Requirements for U.S. visitors vary by country.

Germany

Current State Department guidance says a traveler with a valid U.S. driver's license can drive in Germany for up to six months without obtaining a German license.

Bulgaria

Bulgaria is different.

Current State Department information says a U.S. state driver's license is valid there only when used with an International Driving Permit, and travelers should obtain the IDP in the United States before departure.

Andorra

Andorra currently requires travelers who plan to drive to obtain an IDP before arrival. The State Department also says drivers should carry their driving permit, vehicle documents, and insurance.

Ireland

Ireland may not present the same legal IDP requirement for every visitor, but current U.S. guidance warns that some insurance and car-rental companies may still request one.

These examples show why searching:

Do I need an IDP in Europe?

is too broad.

Search for the exact country you will drive in.

What Is an Inter-American Driving Permit?

You may also come across another term:

Inter-American Driving Permit, or IADP.

This is different from the standard IDP and comes from a separate international agreement covering certain countries in the Americas.

AAA explains that the IADP is issued under the Convention on Regulation of Inter-American Automotive Traffic and, like the IDP, must be used with the traveler's regular driver's license.

AATA also distinguishes between the standard IDP and the Inter-American permit in its current country information.

Do You Need an IADP in South America?

Do not assume that every Central or South American country requires an IADP.

The rules vary, and even current issuer materials contain country-specific conditions.

For example, current State Department guidance says U.S. visitors can usually drive in Argentina with a valid U.S. driver's license. An IDP is not legally required there, although police or rental companies may request one.

The best approach is:

  • ✓
    Check the destination's current driving law.
  • ✓
    Check State Department destination guidance.
  • ✓
    Check whether AAA identifies the standard IDP or IADP for that country.
  • ✓
    Confirm with the rental company.

Do not buy an Inter-American permit simply because you are traveling somewhere in South America.

An IDP Does Not Give You Knew Driving Categories

An International Driving Permit reflects the vehicle categories you are already authorized to drive under your domestic license.

AATA's current information says the permit applies to the vehicle category or categories authorized by the holder's domestic driver's license.

That means an IDP cannot turn a normal car license into permission to drive:

  • ✓
    Motorcycle
  • ✓
    Heavy truck
  • ✓
    Bus
  • ✓
    Other vehicle class you are not licensed for at home

The permit translates your existing driving privileges.

It does not add new ones.

Then check the destination's rules.

Your IDP can reflect an authorized motorcycle category where your domestic license supports it, but it cannot create motorcycle permission that does not exist on your home license.

Also check:

What If Your U.S. Driver's License Expires?

Your IDP does not keep your driving rights alive after your underlying driver's license becomes invalid.

AAA states that an IDP is valid for one year from its effective date provided the state driver's license remains valid.

So if your U.S. license expires during the trip, the IDP should not be treated as a separate valid driver's license.

Before applying, check:

  • ✓
    Driver's-license expiration date
  • ✓
    IDP effective date
  • ✓
    Travel dates
  • ✓
    Length of trip

If your driver's license will expire before or during the trip, deal with the domestic license first.

What If Your Driver's License Is Suspended?

An IDP cannot restore driving privileges that have been suspended or revoked.

It exists as a translation and supplement to a valid domestic driver's license. AAA and AATA both state that the IDP must be used together with a valid U.S. license.

Do not apply for an IDP as a way around a domestic license restriction.

Does a Traffic Ticket Go on Your IDP?

The IDP is not a separate driving record.

AATA says a foreign traffic citation does not attach to the IDP itself. Traffic penalties are handled under the laws of the country where the violation occurred. Serious violations may also affect whether the permit can continue to be used there.

Do not assume that being a tourist protects you from local traffic laws.

You are expected to follow the laws of the country where you drive.

An IDP Is Not Accepted Everywhere

Another mistake is assuming that an IDP gives you worldwide driving permission.

It does not.

Vietnam provides a strong example.

Current State Department guidance says U.S. driver's licenses and U.S.-issued International Driving Permits do not allow U.S. travelers to drive legally in Vietnam. Travelers need the appropriate Vietnamese driving license, and driving without one can lead to fines, arrest, or jail.

So asking:

Do I have an IDP?

is not enough.

You also need to ask:

Does this country accept my type of IDP and U.S. driver's license?

Does an IDP Include Car Insurance?

No.

An International Driving Permit does not provide insurance.

These are completely separate:

IDP = driving-document translation

Insurance = financial protection and legal coverage

The State Department warns that U.S. auto insurance may not cover driving in another country. Even if a U.S. policy provides some coverage, it may not meet the destination's local insurance requirements.

What If Your IDP Is Lost or Stolen?

A lost IDP cannot simply be recreated from a photo stored on your phone.

AAA's current guidance says IDPs are serialized documents. If one is lost or stolen, you need to apply again for a replacement permit rather than use a digital version.

AAA's online IDP FAQ also says a traveler who loses an IDP can reapply through a AAA branch or submit a new application online.

AATA likewise states that there is no digital version of its IDP.

Keep a Copy for Reference

A photocopy or secure photo can help you identify the information if the original is lost.

But it should not be treated as a valid replacement for the physical IDP.

Keep the original secure while traveling.

Can Two Drivers Use the Same IDP?

No.

An IDP is issued to one individual and is linked to that person's domestic driver's license.

If two people plan to drive and both need an IDP, each driver should have their own valid permit.

The same idea applies to the rental agreement.

Make sure every person who plans to drive is listed as an approved driver where the rental contract requires it.

Does an Additional Rental Driver Need an IDP?

If the destination or rental company requires an IDP, each person who will drive should meet that requirement individually.

For example:

Driver 1: license + IDP

Driver 2: license + IDP

One driver's permit does not cover the other driver.

Check additional-driver requirements before leaving the rental desk.

Can You Use an IDP With a Rental Car in Several Countries?

Usually, an IDP can accompany your domestic license across countries that recognize the permit, but that does not automatically mean your rental car can cross every border.

There are two different checks:

Driving Document

How to Apply for an International Driving Permit Step by Step

For U.S. travelers, the safest starting point is one of the two organizations authorized to issue IDPs in the United States:

  • ✓
    American Automobile Association (AAA)
  • ✓
    American Automobile Touring Alliance (AATA)

USAGov confirms both as authorized U.S. IDP issuers. (usa.gov)

The exact application process differs slightly between them, but the basic steps are similar.

Step 1: Confirm That You Actually Need an IDP

Before paying for a permit, check:

  • ✓
    Destination country
  • ✓
    Countries you will drive through
  • ✓
    Rental-company requirements
  • ✓
    Type of vehicle
  • ✓
    Travel dates
  • ✓
    Validity of your U.S. driver's license

Do not get an IDP simply because you are traveling internationally.

Canada and Mexico, for example, currently do not require U.S. citizens visiting with valid U.S. driver's licenses to get an IDP. (usa.gov)

Other countries may require one.

Step 2: Check Your U.S. Driver's License

Your driver's license needs to remain valid while you use the IDP.

Check:

  • ✓
    Expiration date
  • ✓
    Full name
  • ✓
    Vehicle categories
  • ✓
    Current driving status

An IDP is valid only when used with a valid domestic driver's license. (aataidp.com)

If your license will expire before or during the trip, deal with that issue before depending on an IDP.

Step 3: Choose AAA or AATA

You do not need permits from both.

Choose one authorized issuer and follow its current application instructions.

AAA currently offers IDP applications through methods that include in-person, mail, and online options. USAGov also directs travelers to AAA's online, in-person, and mail processes. (usa.gov)

AATA provides an online application process requiring driver's-license information and a passport-style photo. (aataidp.com)

Step 4: Prepare Your Photo

Passport-style photos are used for the permit.

AAA's mail process currently calls for two original passport-style photos, while online application systems can use digital photo submission. (aaa.com)

Follow the photo instructions for the application method you choose rather than assuming normal phone selfies will automatically work.

Step 5: Provide Your Driver's-License Information

You will need proof of your current U.S. driver's license.

For AAA mail applications, current instructions call for a photocopy of the front and back of the license. (aaa.com)

AATA's online process similarly asks applicants to scan or photograph both sides of their current driver's license. (aataidp.com)

Make sure the images are readable.

Step 6: Complete the Application Carefully

Check:

  • ✓
    Full name
  • ✓
    Home address
  • ✓
    Driver's-license information
  • ✓
    Birth information
  • ✓
    Permit effective date
  • ✓
    Vehicle categories
  • ✓
    Travel information where requested

Use the same personal information shown on your driver's license.

Review spelling and dates before submitting.

Step 7: Pay the Permit Fee

The current base permit fee is $20 through AAA and AATA, although photo processing, shipping, or other delivery costs can increase the total. (aaa.com) (aataidp.com)

Check the final amount before submitting because delivery and photo costs can vary.

Step 8: Allow Time for Processing and Delivery

An IDP is a physical document.

Do not apply the night before your flight and expect an electronic permit to appear immediately.

AAA online services currently state that processing can take about five business days plus shipping time. Some AAA clubs recommend submitting online applications at least two weeks before travel. (wa.aaa.com)

AATA also requires processing and delivery time.

Apply early enough that shipping delays do not affect your rental plans.

When Should You Get an IDP Before Travel?

I would check the requirement as soon as I decide that driving may be part of the trip.

You do not necessarily need to apply at that moment.

AAA guidance says an IDP cannot be issued more than six months before the requested effective date. (ace.aaa.com)

A good planning sequence is:

Several Months Before Travel

Check whether each country requires or recognizes an IDP.

Also check your driver's-license expiration date.

One to Two Months Before Travel

Confirm the rental company's document requirements and choose your IDP issuer.

This gives you plenty of room for processing and delivery.

Two to Four Weeks Before Travel

If you have not applied yet, do it.

Online services still require time to process and mail the physical permit.

A Few Days Before Travel

Put the IDP with your other driving documents and check that:

  • ✓
    Effective date covers your trip
  • ✓
    Name is correct
  • ✓
    Vehicle categories are correct
  • ✓
    U.S. driver's license is still valid

Do not pack the permit inside checked luggage.

Choosing the IDP Effective Date

Pay attention to the date when you actually plan to start driving.

Your permit does not need to begin the day you book the trip.

AAA currently states that an IDP can be issued up to six months before the desired effective date. (ace.aaa.com)

Suppose:

  • ✓
    You apply in September
  • ✓
    Your trip begins in December
  • ✓
    You do not plan to drive until December

You may be able to choose an effective date closer to the driving period rather than using unnecessary validity months before travel.

Check the issuer's current instructions when completing the application.

Check Your IDP When It Arrives

Do not put the envelope straight into your travel folder without checking the permit.

Compare it with your U.S. driver's license.

Look at:

  • ✓
    Name
  • ✓
    Photograph
  • ✓
    Effective date
  • ✓
    Expiration date
  • ✓
    Vehicle categories
  • ✓
    Other personal details

If anything appears incorrect, contact the issuer before departure.

It is much easier to correct a document problem at home than at a rental counter overseas.

How Long Can You Use the Same IDP?

Current U.S. guidance says an IDP is valid for one year. (usa.gov)

AATA states that a new IDP application is required each year and that the permit remains valid only when used with a valid U.S. driver's license. (aataidp.com)

AAA guidance also notes that the permit cannot remain useful beyond the validity of the driver's license it accompanies. (ace.aaa.com)

So your usable period may effectively end sooner if your driver's license expires.

Can You Use the Same IDP for Several Trips?

Yes, while it remains valid and the countries involved recognize it.

For example, you might use the same valid permit for trips to several destinations during the year.

But before each trip, check:

  • ✓
    Whether that country recognizes the permit
  • ✓
    Whether local rules have changed
  • ✓
    Whether your domestic license is still valid
  • ✓
    Whether the rental company accepts the documents
  • ✓
    Whether your vehicle category is covered

Do not assume a permit that worked during one vacation automatically meets the rules for the next destination.

Can You Use a U.S. IDP Inside the United States?

No. It is intended to accompany a U.S. driver's license when driving in foreign countries that recognize it.

AATA's current country guidance states that an IDP is not valid in the territory of the country where it was issued. (aataidp.com)

Your regular state driver's license remains your driving document in the United States.

Common International Driving Permit Mistakes

Most IDP problems are easy to avoid if you check the document before leaving home.

1. Treating the IDP as a Driver's License

An IDP does not replace your domestic license.

Carry both.

2. Buying From an Unofficial Website

USA Gov recognizes AAA and AATA as the two authorized U.S. IDP issuers and warns that other websites selling international driver's licenses may be fraudulent. (usa.gov)

Do not choose a seller simply because it appears first in search results.

3. Buying a Digital-Only Permit

A legitimate U.S.-issued IDP is a physical document.

AAA states that IDPs are physically printed rather than issued as digital permits. (aaa.com)

4. Checking Only the Rental Company

The rental desk accepting your documents does not automatically prove that they meet local law.

Check government rules too.

5. Checking Only the Government Rule

The opposite mistake also happens.

A country may accept your driver's license, but the rental company can still ask for an IDP under its own contract.

Check both.

6. Forgetting Other Countries on a Road Trip

A permit requirement can change once you cross a border.

Check every country where you plan to drive.

7. Letting Your U.S. License Expire

The IDP does not remain a standalone license after your domestic driving authority expires.

Check both expiration dates.

8. Assuming the IDP Covers Every Vehicle

Your permit reflects the driving categories authorized by your home license.

It does not add motorcycle, truck, or other vehicle rights you do not already have.

9. Assuming an IDP Works Everywhere

Some countries do not accept a U.S.-issued IDP in the way travelers may expect.

Check the destination individually.

10. Applying Too Late

Online applications still require physical delivery.

Give yourself enough time.

11. Leaving the IDP at the Hotel

If the destination requires it while driving, carry it with your driver's license.

12. Carrying Only a Photo of the IDP

A saved photo is useful as a backup record, but it does not replace the physical permit.

13. Assuming One IDP Covers Two Drivers

Each driver needs their own required driving documents.

Your IDP does not cover your spouse, friend, or additional rental driver.

14. Forgetting Insurance

An IDP does not provide insurance.

Driving permission and insurance are separate requirements.

Final Thoughts

In short, An International Driving Permit is a small document, but it can make a big difference when driving abroad.

The main thing I check is not simply whether a country "uses IDPs." I check whether my U.S. driver's license is accepted, whether an IDP is legally required, whether my rental company asks for one, and whether every country on my driving route follows the same rule.

If you need an IDP, get it from AAA or AATA, carry it with your valid driver's license, and check the dates before departure. USA Gov currently recognizes those two organizations as the authorized U.S. issuers. (usa.gov)

And keep the IDP in its proper role: it helps document your existing driving rights abroad. It does not replace your license, provide insurance, or guarantee that you can legally drive every type of vehicle in every country.

Doing those checks before renting a car can prevent document problems at the rental counter, during a police stop, or after an accident.
