How to Find Cheap Flights: Simple Tips to Save Money (Beginner's Guide)

A practical beginner's guide to comparing airlines, staying flexible, checking nearby airports, using price alerts, and finding better overall value.

✦ Compare more options ✓ Watch the total cost ↕ Save without sacrificing value

When I first started planning trips, I usually booked the first flight ↗ that matched my schedule. I thought all airlines charged similar prices, so there wasn't much reason to compare. After planning a few more trips, I realized that spending just a little extra time searching, comparing airlines, and being flexible with my travel dates could save a surprising amount of money. Since then, finding affordable flights has become one of the first steps in every trip I plan.

Finding cheap flights doesn't mean choosing uncomfortable travel or sacrificing quality. It simply means knowing where to look, when to search, and which booking strategies can help you save. In this beginner's guide, you'll learn why flight prices change, how to compare fares, and simple ways to find cheaper airline tickets without making the process complicated.

Why Do Flight Prices Change?

Many first-time travelers are surprised to see flight prices change from one day to the next.

Airfares are influenced by several factors, including:

  • ✓
    Travel demand.
  • ✓
    Season of the year.
  • ✓
    Seat availability.
  • ✓
    Airline pricing strategies.
  • ✓
    Holidays and special events.

Because these factors change regularly, the price you see today may not be the same tomorrow.

Compare Flights Before You Book

One of the easiest ways to save money is to compare flights instead of booking the first option you find.

Looking at several airlines allows you to:

  • ✓
    Compare ticket prices.
  • ✓
    Review flight schedules.
  • ✓
    Check baggage allowances.
  • ✓
    Compare travel times.
  • ✓
    Find better overall value.

A flight with a slightly higher ticket price may include baggage or other benefits that make it a better deal.

Being flexible gives you more options and may help you:

  • ✓

  • ✓
    Avoid busy travel days.
  • ✓
    Choose less crowded flights.
  • ✓
    Stretch your travel budget further.

Even small changes to your itinerary can sometimes make a noticeable difference.

Focus on the Total Cost

The cheapest ticket isn't always the best value.

Before booking, consider the total cost of your trip, including:

  • ✓
    Checked baggage fees.
  • ✓
    Seat selection charges.
  • ✓
    Airport transfers.
  • ✓
    Meals if they're not included.
  • ✓
    Other optional airline fees.

Looking at the complete cost helps you choose the flight that offers the best overall value instead of simply the lowest advertised price.

Consider Budget Airlines

Budget airlines can be a great option if you're trying to reduce travel costs.

These airlines often offer lower base fares than traditional airlines, making them popular with budget-conscious travelers.

However, before booking, check whether the ticket includes:

  • ✓
    Carry-on baggage.
  • ✓
    Checked baggage.
  • ✓
    Seat selection.
  • ✓
    Meals or drinks.
  • ✓
    Flight changes.

Sometimes a slightly higher-priced ticket with more included services can provide better overall value.

Set Up Price Alerts

If your travel dates are still flexible, consider setting up price alerts.

Many flight search platforms allow you to track a route and notify you if the fare changes.

Price alerts can help you:

  • ✓
    Monitor airfare changes.
  • ✓
    Compare prices over time.
  • ✓
    Avoid checking prices every day.
  • ✓
    Book when the fare fits your budget.

This is a simple way to stay informed without constantly searching.

Flights may be less expensive when:

  • ✓

Before booking, check whether the airline charges extra for:

  • ✓
    Carry-on luggage.
  • ✓
    Seat selection.
  • ✓
    Priority boarding.
  • ✓
    Flight changes.
  • ✓
    Food and drinks on board.

Adding these costs together gives you a clearer picture of the total price.

Common Mistakes When Looking for Cheap Flights

Finding cheap flights isn't difficult, but a few common mistakes can stop you from getting the best deal.

From my own experience, I used to book the first affordable ticket I found because I was worried the price would increase. Later, I realized that taking a little extra time to compare airlines, review the total cost, and check nearby airports often helped me find a much better option.

Some of the most common mistakes include:

  • ✓
    Booking the first flight you find.
  • ✓
    Ignoring baggage fees and other extra charges.
  • ✓
    Searching only one airline or website.
  • ✓
    Not comparing nearby airports.
  • ✓
    Choosing inconvenient flight times just because they're cheaper.
  • ✓
    Waiting until the last minute to start searching.
  • ✓
    Forgetting to check the total travel time.
  • ✓
    Not reading the ticket's terms and conditions.

Avoiding these mistakes can help you save money without creating unnecessary travel problems.
